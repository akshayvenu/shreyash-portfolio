Section 03 — how one designer covered six products

The method

1 designer
6 portals
01
Audit before design

On existing products I took knowledge transfer from developers and walked every screen myself, listing the gaps a dev-first build had skipped.

02
Write the system first

Every product got a written guideline — users, tokens, component rules, icon sources — before a single screen. That document is why six products don't look like six accidents.

03
Paper, then prompts

Flows start on paper. Then a phased, screen-by-screen prompt sequence drives high-fidelity output. AI accelerated the execution; the framing and the decisions stayed mine.

04
Stay past handoff

I packaged implementation-ready artifacts and sat with developers through the build, answering the questions specs never cover.
