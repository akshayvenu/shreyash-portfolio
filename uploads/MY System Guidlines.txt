## What Is LearningVision

LearningVision is an AI-powered learning experience
platform that unifies training content, intelligent
tutoring, adaptive learning journeys, expert networks,
and complete skill tracking into one seamless system.

## Modules (5 total):

1. Learning Management System (LMS)
2. TutorBot (AI-Assisted)
3. AI-Assisted Learning Journey
4. Expert Network
5. Skill Management

## Who Uses This Portal

- Primary: Professors / Instructors / Trainers
- Secondary: Supervisors / Department Heads
- Age: 28–55 years
- Context: Corporate L&D and academic institutions
- Device: Desktop primary
- Mindset: Structured, process-oriented, professional
  Needs efficiency and clear workflows

## Design Philosophy

Clean, warm, professional EdTech.
Pastel backgrounds, white cards, red accents.
Think: Canvas LMS + Notion + Google Classroom
BUT more polished, more premium, more AI-forward.
Warm pastels make it feel approachable not cold.
Every screen should feel organized and trustworthy.
